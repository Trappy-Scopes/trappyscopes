
# Coin Tossing - the "Hello World" of experiments

Every complicated thing requires a simple example to understand. To understand an experiment orcastration system, we will need a simple experiment. The simpleset example that I can think of is a "coin toss" experiment. Toss a coin and record the outcome (either HEADS or TAILS). A fair coin should record a 50% detection of either.

The `scripts/toyexp/cointoss` contains a series of scripts that progressively get complicated and illustrate the different capabilities of the system.
